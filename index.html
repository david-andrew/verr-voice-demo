Listen with

Solid symbols are the singers’ vowels, drawn where they actually sound (red ones occur in this score). Faint symbols mark the textbook IPA spots. Every spot sings what its position says. The palest symbols need lip rounding, which isn’t available yet.

Change it

Schwa [ə] in specific words

Choosing [ə] above changes the plain [ə], including choral “the” and “a”. Words that a diction rule sings differently (American “the” → [ʌ], verb “-eth” → [ɛ], “-es” → [ɪ]…) are listed here; give any word its own sound from the dot.

Language profiles (vowels and pronunciations)

A profile holds vowel changes you want in many pieces (part-only changes stay with their piece). Save this piece’s changes into the profile it uses, or as a new one; load one into another piece, or make one the default for its language.
