The generated browser bank contains derived WORLD spectral envelopes,
aperiodicity, pitch motion, and curated articulation analyses of public
VocalSet recordings. The original recordings are not included in this checkout.
Changes: register segmentation, spectral analysis, constant unit calibration,
consonant trajectory extraction and native float32 packing. See the generated
bank.json for source SHA-256 hashes and analysis versions.

Articulation context selection also references Annotated VocalSet:
Faghih & Timoney (2022), https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.7061507.
No private recordings, fitted voice profiles, or private scores are in the bank.
